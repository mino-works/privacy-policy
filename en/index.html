Ikatama! Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

This page explains how the iPhone and iPad app "Ikatama!" (App Store name: "Ikatama: Ocean Merge Puzzle", Japanese name: いかたま！) handles information. In this policy, "the app" means Ikatama!, and "we" or "us" means mino works, the developer and operator of the app, based in Japan.

1. Summary

  • No account or sign-in. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, photos or contacts.
  • We do not run servers for the app, and the app does not send your game data to us.
  • Your scores and progress are stored only on your device.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device information to show and measure ads, as described below. You can say no to tracking and, where the law requires it, to personalized ads.
  • If you buy "Remove Ads", the app stops loading ads and does not start the Google ads service.

2. Information stored only on your device

The app stores the following on your device only: best scores for each stage, which stages you have played or cleared, whether the app has asked you for a review, whether you have bought "Remove Ads", and your answers to the consent messages (see section 4). This information is not sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this information.

3. Ads (Google AdMob)

The app is free to play because it shows ads from Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. It shows full-screen ads and video ads that you choose to watch, for example to retry a stage.

To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google AdMob may collect and use:

  • the advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking (section 5)
  • IP address, device type, operating system version, language and similar device information
  • interactions with ads, such as views and taps
  • diagnostic information, such as crash data

Google handles this information under its own policies. See Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

4. Consent messages (Google User Messaging Platform)

The app uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for and record your privacy choices for ads.

  • United Kingdom, Switzerland and the European Economic Area: Before ads are loaded, the app shows a consent message. You can consent, or decline. If you decline, you may still see ads, but they will not be personalized, and Google uses limited data.
  • U.S. states with privacy laws (for example, California): You can opt out of the use of your information for personalized ads (which some state laws call "selling" or "sharing" personal information, or "targeted advertising").
  • Changing your choice: Where these laws apply, a "Privacy Choices" link appears on the title screen of the app. Tap it at any time to see the message again and change your choice.

Your choice is stored on your device, and Google also receives it so that ads follow your choice.

5. Tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency)

When you open the app for the first time after installing it, the app shows the iPhone's "Allow tracking" prompt, with a short explanation of why the app asks.

  • If you allow: the advertising identifier (IDFA) is shared with Google AdMob, and ads may better match your interests.
  • If you do not allow: the IDFA is not shared. You still see ads, but they are not based on tracking. The game works the same.

You can change this at any time in the iPhone Settings app: Privacy & Security, then Tracking.

6. In-app purchase (Remove Ads)

The app offers one optional, one-time purchase called "Remove Ads". You can play every stage without buying it.

  • Purchases and payments are handled by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, payment details or Apple Account information.
  • Whether you have bought it is stored only on your device.
  • After you buy it, the app shows no ads and retries work without videos. From the next launch, the app does not start Google AdMob at all, so the information in section 3 is not sent to Google AdMob, and the consent and tracking messages are not shown.
  • If you delete the app or change devices, tap "Restore Purchase" on the title screen with the same Apple Account.

Refunds follow Apple's rules.

7. Legal basis (UK, Switzerland and EEA users)

We do not process your personal data on our own servers. For the ads in the app:

  • personalized ads, and storing or reading information on your device for ads, are based on your consent (section 4), which you can withdraw at any time;
  • non-personalized ads and fraud prevention are based on legitimate interests, so that the app can be offered for free.

Google acts as an independent controller for the data it collects through AdMob. Google may transfer data to the United States and other countries using the safeguards described in its privacy policy.

8. Your rights

UK, Switzerland and EEA

You have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, to object to its use, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier use. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office).

U.S. state privacy laws (California and other states)

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to know what personal information is collected, to access and delete it, to correct it, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information and of targeted advertising. We will not treat you differently for using these rights.

In the last 12 months, the categories of information collected through the app, all by Google AdMob for advertising, are: identifiers (IDFA if allowed, IP address), internet or other electronic activity (ad interactions) and device information. We do not sell personal information for money. We do not collect sensitive personal information.

How to use your rights

  • Ads choices: use "Privacy Choices" in the app (section 4) and the tracking setting (section 5).
  • Information on your device: delete the app.
  • Information held by Google: see Google Privacy Policy and My Ad Center.
  • Any other request or question: contact us (section 13). Because we hold no personal data on servers, we usually cannot identify you, but we will help you with your request to Google.

9. Children

The app is a general audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us.

10. Sharing

We do not sell or give your information to third parties. As described in sections 3 and 4, Google AdMob may collect device information for ads.

11. Features the app does not use

The app does not use notifications, location, camera, microphone, photos or contacts.

12. Links and review requests

Tapping "Privacy Policy" on the title screen opens this page in your web browser. After you clear a stage, the app may use the iPhone's standard feature to ask you to rate it on the App Store. We do not receive the content of your rating.

13. Contact

Operator: mino works (Japan). For questions about this policy or your privacy, please contact us through the "App Support" link on the app's App Store page.

14. Changes to this policy

We will update this page when the app's features change. For important changes, we update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
